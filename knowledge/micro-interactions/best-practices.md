# Micro-interaction best practices

> Sources: NN/g, Dan Saffer / Smart Design, IxDF, Material Design, Emil Kowalski, W3C WCAG, web.dev (compiled 2026-10-21)
> Raw: [web research summary](../raw/micro-interactions/2026-10-21-web-research-summary.md)
> Updated: 2026-10-21

## Summary

Best micro-interactions are short trigger–feedback pairs that show status, prevent errors, and improve perceived speed—not decorative delight. Use Saffer’s Trigger → Rules → Feedback → Loops/Modes. Keep routine chrome under ~300ms on transform/opacity; honor `prefers-reduced-motion`. Ship acknowledgement, wait ladders, instant toggles, form validation, and undo before brand delight.
