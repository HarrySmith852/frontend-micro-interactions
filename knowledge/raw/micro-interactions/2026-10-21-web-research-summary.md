# Web research summary — micro-interactions (2026-10-21)

Compiled from research notes in `research_notes/Best micro interaction practices/` and report `reports/Best micro interaction practices.md`.

## Load-bearing claims (with sources in report)

1. Micro-interactions = contained product moments that do one small task (Dan Saffer); NN/g = trigger–feedback pairs.
2. Four parts: Trigger, Rules, Feedback, Loops/Modes.
3. Response-time limits: ≤0.1s instant; ≤1s thought flow; ≤10s attention (NN/g).
4. Routine UI motion typically under ~300ms; press ~100–160ms (Emil Kowalski / NN/g).
5. Animate transform/opacity; never transition:all (web.dev).
6. Honor prefers-reduced-motion; WCAG 2.3.3 for interaction-triggered motion (W3C).
7. Highest ROI: acknowledgement, wait ladders, instant toggles, well-timed form validation, undo — delight last.
8. Decorative / entertainment animation during waits often hurts UX (NN/g).
